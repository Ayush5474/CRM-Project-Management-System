# CRM – Project Management System

## Project Overview

The **CRM Project Management System** is a centralized, end-to-end project lifecycle management platform designed to manage and coordinate business projects from initial proposal creation to development, testing, and delivery.

The system connects multiple organizational teams, including IT Sales, Data Analytics (DA), Business Analysts (BA), Designers, Developers, and Testers, within a unified project management environment.

It provides structured management of client inquiries, requirements gathering, project feasibility, proposal creation, quotations, project planning, resource allocation, development activities, timelines, cost sheets, pricing, and project progress.

The platform focuses on improving cross-team coordination, maintaining project transparency, managing resources and costs, and ensuring structured execution throughout the complete project lifecycle.

## Core Modules

- **Inquiry & Lead Management:** Registration and tracking of client inquiries, business requirements, and potential projects.
- **Requirement Management:** Collection and documentation of client requirements, scope definition, business requirements, and supporting documents.
- **Feasibility & Evaluation:** Technical feasibility assessment, internal evaluation, resource availability, and project validation.
- **Proposal Management:** Creation and management of project proposals, scope of work, deliverables, and commercial documentation.
- **Quotation & Pricing:** Preparation of quotations, pricing structures, cost estimates, and commercial calculations.
- **Project Planning:** Project initiation, milestone definition, timelines, resource planning, dependencies, and execution schedules.
- **Business Analysis (BA):** Requirement analysis, BRD preparation, process documentation, workflow definition, and stakeholder coordination.
- **Design Management:** UI/UX design planning, design assignments, prototype management, review cycles, and design approvals.
- **Development Management:** Developer allocation, development task tracking, implementation progress, deadlines, and technical coordination.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance:** Tester allocation, test planning, issue tracking, bug reporting, and testing status management.
- **Timeline Management:** Tracking project schedules, milestones, task deadlines, delays, and revised completion dates.
- **Cost Sheet Management:** Tracking estimated and actual project costs, resource costs, development expenses, and financial planning.
- **Resource Management:** Coordination and allocation of employees across IT Sales, DA & Analytics, BA, Design, Development, and Testing teams.
- **Project Documentation:** Centralized management of BRDs, flowcharts, proposals, designs, technical documents, and project-related files.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Project progress, team performance, timelines, cost analysis, resource utilization, and overall project reporting.

## Participating Teams

The system brings multiple departments together under one project lifecycle.

| Team | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| IT Sales Team | Client inquiries, requirement coordination, proposals, quotations, pricing, and commercial discussions. |
| DA & Analytics Team | Project coordination, analytics-related requirements, reporting, and analytical deliverables. |
| Business Analysts (BA) | Requirement gathering, BRDs, process flows, documentation, and stakeholder coordination. |
| Design Team | UI/UX design, prototypes, design reviews, and design approvals. |
| Development Team | Technical implementation, developer allocation, development planning, and execution. |
| Testing Team | Quality assurance, test execution, bug identification, validation, and testing sign-off. |

## Project Lifecycle

The system follows a structured project execution process:

1. Client Inquiry Registration
2. Requirement Gathering
3. Project Scoping
4. Technical Feasibility Assessment
5. Internal Evaluation
6. Proposal and Quotation Creation
7. Commercial Approval and Confirmation
8. Project Documentation
9. Business Analysis and Design
10. Design Review and Approval
11. Development Planning and Resource Allocation
12. Software Development
13. Testing and Quality Assurance
14. Client UAT and Validation
15. Project Delivery and Go-Live

## Important Implementation Notes

- The system covers the project lifecycle from initial client inquiry and proposal creation through development and testing.
- Multiple departments work within the same project environment, with responsibilities distributed according to their respective functions.
- Project timelines, resource allocation, cost sheets, and pricing are managed as part of project planning and execution.
- Business requirements, design documentation, and technical deliverables are maintained within the project lifecycle.
- Development and testing activities are tracked to provide visibility into project execution and delivery readiness.
- Detailed workflows, dependencies, and approval rules are maintained in separate project documentation.
